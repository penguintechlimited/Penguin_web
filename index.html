07 FAQ

Common questions.

How long does a typical web project take?

Landing pages ship in 5-7 business days. Corporate websites take 2-4 weeks. Full e-commerce platforms run 4-8 weeks depending on complexity. Every engagement begins with a signed scope — no scope creep, no surprises.

Do you work with clients outside Bangladesh?

Yes — we serve clients across 12 countries and operate fully async. Payments processed in USD, GBP, and EUR via Stripe. We align to your timezone for strategy calls and deliver work asynchronously.

What is your minimum engagement?

Project-based work starts at $250 for a landing page. Marketing retainers start at $350/month. There is no lock-in on retainers — monthly rolling with 30-day notice. We earn your renewal every month.

Can you handle both the build and the marketing?

That is our primary model — the Ecosystem Flywheel. Clients who combine a web/app build with a marketing retainer see significantly better ROI because the tech stack is engineered to support the growth channels from the ground up. No integration friction.

Who owns the final deliverables?

You do. All code, design assets, and ad accounts are fully transferred to you upon final payment. We hand over full admin access to everything — repositories, hosting, ad accounts, and analytics.
