07 Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions.

How do payments work for projects in Bangladesh?

We work on a clear milestone schedule: 50% upfront to kick off architecture and design, and 50% upon completed testing and deployment. We accept bKash Merchant, Nagad, direct bank transfer, and international cards.

Do your web and mobile apps include bKash and Nagad payment gateways?

Yes. We specialize in local fintech integrations including bKash Tokenized/PGW, Nagad API, Rocket, Upay, and SSL Commerz / Shurjopay card gateways with automated callback validation and webhook reconciliation.

How long does an average website or app take to build?

Landing pages take 3–5 business days. Full corporate sites take 2–3 weeks. E-commerce platforms take 3–5 weeks. Mobile apps range between 6–14 weeks depending on feature complexity and third-party API requirements.

Can Penguin Tech manage our monthly digital advertising?

Yes. We offer dedicated monthly performance retainers starting at ৳8,000/mo for social management and ৳20,000/mo for full-funnel Meta and Google Ads management with weekly conversion tracking.

Who owns the intellectual property and code?

You own 100% of all intellectual property, source code, Figma design files, database architectures, and marketing accounts. We transfer complete repository access and master credentials upon final delivery.
